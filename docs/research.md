# Target selection, 24 October 2026

Alteryx, new data-preparation family. Scores in order: annual bill 3, data-shaped 5, buyer fit 2, search demand 4, one-session buildable 4, import path 5. Total 23/30.

[Alteryx pricing](https://www.alteryx.com/platform/pricing) lists Starter at USD 250 per user per month billed annually, with 1 to 10 seats. Ten seats give an annual list-price scenario of USD 30,000. No verified NZ/AU customer invoice was found in permitted local customer or CRM snapshots. Buyer fit is therefore scored conservatively. Professional and Enterprise have no public figure. Old USD 5,195 Designer figures in third-party lists were not used.

[Matillion](https://www.matillion.com/pricing) and [KNIME](https://www.knime.com/pricing) were checked as other candidates. Their current enterprise pricing is quoted. Without a verified five-figure annual bill each scored 1/5/2/3/4/4, total 19/30. KNIME also has a free desktop platform, making the expensive-software argument weaker.

[Alternative-search results](https://www.g2.com/products/alteryx/competitors/alternatives) show established comparison demand. This is qualitative evidence, not measured search volume or a claim about acquisition outcomes.

Local partner-discovery records contained prospective vendor programmes, not an existing resale agreement. No evidence found that Enterprise DNA resells Alteryx. No live CRM or customer repository was accessed.

CSV data export uses [Alteryx Output Data](https://help.alteryx.com/aac/en/designer-experience/designer-cloud-tool-list/cloud-native-mode-tools/workflow-in-out-tools/output-data-tool.html). Saved workflow files are a different format and are not imported by this build. The implementation and page state that boundary.
